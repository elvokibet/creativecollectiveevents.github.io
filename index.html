Our mission

More Than Events. We Create Opportunities.

At Creative Collective Events, we believe every small business deserves an opportunity to be discovered. Our markets bring entrepreneurs face to face with shoppers who appreciate originality, craftsmanship, and the stories behind the products they purchase.

From handmade creations and independent brands to specialty foods and innovative lifestyle products, we create welcoming spaces where businesses can showcase their work, build relationships, gain visibility, and connect with their communities.

Every event is an opportunity to discover something special, support an entrepreneur, and strengthen the local business community.
